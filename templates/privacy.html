Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to InstaDown ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting and respecting your digital privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how InstaDown handles your data when you visit and use our web-based Instagram media downloader utility at https://instagram-story-downloader.onrender.com/.

2. Core Privacy Principles

  • No Account Required: You can use InstaDown freely without creating an account or providing your email address, phone number, or name.
  • No Instagram Credentials: We never ask for your Instagram username, password, two-factor authentication codes, or session tokens.
  • No Permanent Storage of Media: When you download an Instagram reel, video, or photo, the file is retrieved ephemerally into temporary memory solely to stream it to your client browser. It is purged immediately upon completion.

3. Information We Process Ephemerally

When you submit an Instagram link into the input field, our server receives the public URL to extract the media identifier (shortcode) and fetch publicly accessible metadata from Instagram's public endpoints. The processing works as follows:

  • The public URL submitted by you.
  • A temporary scratch folder allocated dynamically for the duration of the download.
  • Instant automatic deletion of all downloaded files once the HTTP download response is finished.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

InstaDown utilizes localStorage purely to remember your preferences, such as your cookie consent choices (e.g. instadown_cookie_consent). We do not use intrusive tracking cookies, tracking beacons, or cross-site tracking pixels.

5. Third-Party Services & Links

Our website interfaces with external platforms:

  • Instagram (Meta Platforms, Inc.): Media URLs and thumbnails originate from Instagram CDN servers. Your browser may establish direct network requests to display Instagram thumbnails.
  • GitHub: Source code is hosted on GitHub for open-source transparency.
  • Analytics (Optional): If configured by the site administrator and consented to by you, anonymous page usage statistics may be gathered to understand traffic and optimize performance.

6. Data Security

We enforce modern security best practices across all traffic:

  • Mandatory HTTPS: All communication between your device and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • HSTS & Security Headers: We enforce HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN, and modern Referrer Policies.

7. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Because we do not store personal identification data, profile databases, or media libraries, we do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to data brokers or advertisers.
  • You have the right to visit our website anonymously, decline non-essential cookies, or clear your browser's local cache at any time.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our operational procedures or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, you can open an issue or reach out via our official GitHub repository: github.com/KisholoyDD21/Instagram-story-Downloader.
