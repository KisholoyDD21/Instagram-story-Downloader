How to Download Instagram Stories & Highlights

By default, Instagram Stories and Highlights are protected by Instagram's user session and private API restrictions. To save a story:

  1. Ensure the creator's account is completely Public.
  2. Tap the three dots on the story or highlight and select Copy Link.
  3. Paste the link into the box above and click Fetch Media.
Works exclusively for public Instagram accounts. Private accounts require account login and are not supported to protect user privacy. All rights belong to respective content owners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract background audio from Reels?

Yes. InstaDown includes a built-in audio extraction engine. Simply click "Extract Audio (MP3)" on any Reel to download the audio track directly.

Can I download multi-slide carousel albums?

Yes! InstaDown parses every slide in a carousel. You can preview individual slides, download them one by one, or download the entire album as a ZIP file.

How does 'Send to Phone' work?

Clicking "Send to Phone (QR)" displays an instant QR code. Scan it with your phone's camera app to open and save the media directly onto your mobile device without retyping URLs.

Are downloaded files watermarked?

No. InstaDown delivers raw, high-definition media files directly from Instagram's content delivery network with zero added watermarks or logos.
